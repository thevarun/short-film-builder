How ShortFilmBuilder makes a film

Twelve stages from a written story to a rendered short. Each ends at a gate: you look, or listen, and say yes before the next stage spends anything. Click a stage to see who does what, what it produces, and what went wrong the first time.

You decide and approve Agent plans, writes, paints, reviews Script does the paid, deterministic work
01Read & castWho is in this story, and what do they look like?AgentYou

Produces

character-bible.md: every character, canonical heights, a locked style anchor, proportion rules by subject class.

Cost

Nothing yet.

GateThe author's intent is confirmed and the style anchor image is locked.
First time: a style rule that said "large heads, small bodies" for everyone drew the adult as a child through three rounds. Rules now attach to a subject class.
02Character sheetsOne reference sheet per character, on model.AgentYou

Produces

art/characters/*.png

Cost

About a dollar of image generations.

GateEach sheet approved. Adults are generated fresh, never edited from a child's sheet.
03Beat sheetThe story as beats, with the author's own lines kept verbatim.AgentYou

Produces

beat-sheet.md: beats, verbatim lines, a runtime budget, and the one world rule that governs every visual choice.

Cost

Nothing.

GateRuntime and every adaptation decision agreed with you.
04Location bibleOne master plate per place.AgentYou

Produces

art/locations/*.png, with a colour-temperature progression across the film.

Cost

About a dollar.

GatePlates approved.
05Audio scriptEvery line, pre-normalised, with performance tags and a cast.AgentScriptYou

Produces

audio-script-*.md and audio/script-*.json. Candidate voices are auditioned with voice-audition.ts.

Cost

Cents, for the auditions.

GateVoices chosen; the script read once for line changes.
06Record & alignOne continuous take, then measure exactly where every sentence is.ScriptYou

Produces

The mp3, and audio/*.sentences.json with the measured start and end of every sentence. record-dialogue → force-align → split-sentences → align-sentences, in that order.

Cost

Under a dollar.

GateYou listen to the take and accept the runtime. The recording is now the timeline; nothing is cut before this.
First time: the voice API's own timestamps ran 4% short of the audio it returned. Every cut drifted, and three "fixes" were verified against the wrong ruler. Now the saved mp3 is force-aligned and it alone is the ruler.
07Shot planWhich sentences each shot owns, and how shots join.AgentYou

Produces

shot-plan-*.md: shots cut to sentence spans, live clip or still-with-camera-move, transitions, the paintings needed.

Cost

Nothing.

GatePlan confirmed before any plate is painted.
First time: beats were split into equal thirds, and a dragon appeared on screen under "had never seen a dragon". Shots are cut to sentences now, never to fractions of a beat.
08PlatesThe first frame of every shot, painted in story order.AgentYou

Produces

art/shots/*.png, each referencing the last for continuity.

Cost

About $8 for forty shots, including rejects.

GateThe plate audit: no props from a later beat, no characters not yet introduced, legible at the framing, nothing that breaks the world rule.
First time: the heroine carried a sword nineteen seconds before she was given it, painted into four plates. A prompt cannot delete what the first frame contains; the plate is fixed instead.
09Shot specsWhat moves, and how the camera moves, one of each per clip.AgentScript

Produces

motion/motion-*.json: action, camera, constraints, per-shot negatives, frame chains; and the cut in film.json.

Cost

Nothing.

Gatecheck-retime passes: every clip will play between 0.5× and 1.3×, and the music will land.
First time: writing "no fire" in the prompt body made the fire bigger. Constraints are affirmative in the body; bans live only in the negative prompt.
10GenerateClips from the plates, reviewed one contact strip at a time.ScriptAgentYou

Produces

cache/motion/*.mp4 at the lite tier, every take archived by content hash; strips from build-strips.

Cost

About $30 for forty shots. Roughly a third of clips need a second roll.

GateEvery clip reviewed as a strip against the audit rules; what fails is re-rolled, and when the plate is the cause, the plate is repainted first.
First time: a pull-back through a frame the model had to invent re-staged the scene with two of the same character. Cameras stay locked in crowds; real moves are saved for simple frames.
11Compose & renderArcs first, then parts, then the film.Script

Produces

Remotion compositions generated from film.json; rendered files under out/.

Cost

Nothing. Renders are local.

Gateverify-sync and verify-picture pass on the rendered file: every cut within a quarter second of a speech onset, no black frames where a shot should be.
First time: a trimmed clip went black for the last third of its window and every data-level check passed. The checks now look at the frames and the audio of the file itself.
12FinishTitle, credits, music, and the whole thing watched once.AgentScriptYou

Produces

Cards and credits in film.json; a composed instrumental cue placed so its last strong bars land under the end card; levels measured.

Cost

About a dollar for the cue.

GateYou watch the whole film.
First time: the end card was silent three times running, because the cue's fade was pinned instead of its landing. The script now measures where the chord lands.
